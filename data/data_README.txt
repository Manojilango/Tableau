CORD-19 Research Trends Dashboard - Data Files
Author: Manoj Kumar Ilango
FA 550 Data Visualization - Capstone Project

FILES:
cord19_master.csv - Full cleaned dataset (499,714 unique papers)
monthly_volume.csv - Paper count by year and month (397 rows)
yearly_volume.csv - Paper count aggregated by year (34 rows)
top_journals.csv - Top 20 journals by paper count (20 rows)
source_breakdown.csv - Paper count by source collection (48 rows)
journal_year.csv - Top 15 journals by paper count per year (147 rows)
kpi_summary.csv - Single-row KPI metrics table (1 row)
top_keywords.csv - Top 100 keywords from paper titles (100 rows)

SOURCE: CORD-19 Dataset - Allen Institute for AI
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/allen-institute-for-ai/CORD-19-research-challenge
